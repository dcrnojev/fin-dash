fin-dash privacy policy

fin-dash is a personal finance dashboard that keeps a household's figures in its own Google Spreadsheet.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for your name, email address and the drive.file permission. That permission covers only spreadsheets the app created for you or that you chose in the Google file picker. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Where your data goes

  • The app runs entirely in your browser. It reads and writes your spreadsheet directly with Google; there is no server of ours in between, and no copy of your data is kept anywhere else.
  • Your email address is written into the spreadsheet when you close a month, to record who closed it.
  • Your browser keeps the spreadsheet's id, your email address and the "Hide amounts" choice on your device, and the Google access token only for the open tab. Signing out removes the token and the email address.
  • There are no analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party scripts other than Google's own sign-in and file picker.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to show and update your own spreadsheet, and is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account connections. Your spreadsheet stays in your Drive, and deleting it there removes the data.

Contact

Questions go to the issues of the fin-dash repository.
